ABOUT US

"Translation Board" LLC, established in 2023, is a professional translation agency providing high-quality linguistic services to clients in Mongolia and abroad in accordance with international standards of translation quality and professional ethics. The company specializes in written translation services across a wide range of subject areas and document types, ensuring accuracy, confidentiality, consistency, and professional responsibility at every stage of the translation process. Our work is guided by the principles of quality assurance, operational efficiency, and an individual approach to each client and project. Our team includes qualified translators of Mongolian, Russian and English, as well as experienced freelance specialists in Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. To maintain the highest standards of service, the company applies a strict double quality control system. Every completed translation undergoes a mandatory review and final verification by the chief translator prior to delivery to the client. This process ensures linguistic accuracy, terminological consistency, and full correspondence with the original document. Translation Board LLC operates under an official business license and uses the official seal of the Certified Translation Association of Mongolia, confirming the company’s legal standing and professional accreditation within the translation industry. The company is headed by a professional translator from the Russian Federation who holds a Bachelor’s degree in Linguistics from Transbaikal State University and a Master’s degree from the National University of Mongolia. Translation Board LLC is committed to building long-term professional relationships based on trust, reliability, and service excellence. We strive to provide accurate and dependable translation solutions for private individuals, corporate clients, governmental institutions, and international partners.

QUALITY CONTROL
